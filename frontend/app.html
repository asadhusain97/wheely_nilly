Glossary

Definitions for portfolio performance, capital, contracts, strategy settings, and Radar terms.

Options basics

Option contract Call or put
A deal to buy or sell shares at a set price. One contract usually covers 100 shares.
Learn more
Covered call CC
You own shares. You get paid now for agreeing to sell them at the strike price.
Learn more
Cash-secured put CSP
You get paid now for agreeing to buy shares. You keep enough cash ready for that purchase.
Learn more
Premium Option price
The price of an option. Sellers collect it. Buyers pay it.
Learn more
ITM / ATM / OTM Moneyness state
ITM has value now. ATM is near the stock price. OTM has no value if exercised now.
Learn more
Intrinsic & extrinsic value What premium contains
Intrinsic is value right now. Extrinsic is what people pay for time and possibility.
Learn more
Exercise & assignment
Exercise: the buyer uses the deal. Assignment: the seller must keep their side of it.
Learn more

Strategy settings

Goals & ticker changes Settings layers
A goal sets the defaults. A ticker change replaces a default for just one stock.
Learn more
Goal profiles Keep Shares · Earn Income · Plan Exit · Plan Entry
Ready-made settings for keeping shares, earning income, selling shares, or buying shares.
Learn more
DTE range Time window
How many days can be left before an option expires. Radar stays inside this range.
Learn more
Roll review DTE Management window
The days-left point when Wheely Nilly reminds you to review a possible roll.
Learn more
Moneyness Strike / stock
How the strike price compares with the stock price. 100% means they match.
Learn more
Target delta range Absolute delta
The lowest and highest delta Radar may accept. A −0.25 put counts as 0.25.
Learn more
Maximum spread Liquidity rule
The biggest gap allowed between the bid and ask. Smaller gaps usually mean easier trading.
Learn more
Minimum open interest Liquidity rule
The fewest open contracts Radar will accept. More can mean more buyers and sellers.
Learn more
Minimum volume Liquidity rule
The fewest contracts that must have traded today. The count starts over each trading day.
Learn more
Minimum return One option period
The smallest return Radar may show. It covers this option's life, not a whole year.
Learn more
Net price guard Ticker setting
Your do-not-cross price. It hides calls that sell too low and puts that buy too high.
Learn more

Performance

Booked profit Booked P&L
Money made or lost on finished option trades, after recorded fees. Positive means profit.
Opening net credit + closing net cash flow
Learn more
Return on collateral Return
Booked profit compared with the money tied up in those trades.
Booked profit ÷ entry collateral
Learn more
Annualized return Annualized rate
A short-term return stretched to a one-year pace. It helps compare trades. It is not a forecast.
Closed trades = booked profit × 365 ÷ Σ(collateral × days held)
Radar = return on capital × 365 ÷ DTE
Learn more
Remaining annualized return
The one-year pace of the option value still left to earn. It is an estimate, not a promise.
Remaining extrinsic ÷ capital at risk × 365 ÷ max(DTE, 1)
Learn more
Capital velocity
Profit made per $1,000 tied up, adjusted to a 30-day pace.
Booked profit ÷ collateral-days × 30 × $1,000
Learn more
Premium capture
How much of the premium you have kept. 50% means you kept half.
Open trade = profit if closed ÷ opening net credit
Dashboard = booked profit ÷ positive opening credits
Learn more
Monthly booked P&L
Finished option profits and losses grouped by closing month and stock.
Learn more

Capital & contracts

Wheel capital
Money tied up in wheel trades: cash behind puts plus the cost of covered shares.
Open CSP collateral + share capital
Learn more
CSP collateral Cash secured
Cash kept ready in case your put makes you buy the shares.
Strike × contract multiplier × open contracts
Learn more
Share capital Shares committed
What you paid for the shares being used by covered calls.
Broker cost basis per share × covered shares
Learn more
Collateral Ticker view
Money or shares set aside to back a trade.
CSP collateral + share capital
Learn more
Open CSPs / open CCs
Your cash-secured puts and covered calls that have not ended yet.
Learn more
Contract multiplier
Shares controlled by one contract. Usually 100, but some adjusted options differ.
Learn more
Premium received
Cash collected when you sold the option, after recorded opening fees.
Gross opening credit − recorded opening fees
Learn more
Buyback debit
Estimated cost to buy back and close your option now, including an estimated fee.
Ask × multiplier × contracts + estimated closing fees
Learn more
Profit if closed
What the option might make or lose if you closed it now. The quote can change.
Premium received − buyback debit
Learn more
Earned per day
Estimated profit so far, divided by days held. It does not predict tomorrow.
Profit if closed ÷ days held
Learn more
Extrinsic per day
Time value still left, divided by days left. Real time decay is not this smooth.
Remaining extrinsic ÷ max(DTE, 1)
Learn more
Closing cash flow Close cost
Money paid or received when an option closes. A buyback shows as money out.
Learn more
DTE Days to expiration
Calendar days until the option ends. Zero means it ends today.
Learn more
Stock price
The latest stock quote available to Wheely Nilly.
Learn more

Radar opportunities

Candidate rank
How closely a contract matches your settings. Rank 1 is the closest match, not the safest trade.
Learn more
Strike price
The set share price written into the option deal.
Learn more
Underlying price
The stock or fund price used to calculate the option numbers.
Learn more
Executable option price Midpoint · Bid · Ask
The price used for an estimate. Radar may use the midpoint. A real order may fill elsewhere.
Learn more
Bid-ask spread Quote width
The gap between what buyers offer and sellers ask. A smaller gap is usually easier to trade.
Learn more
Open interest / volume Liquidity activity
Open interest means contracts still open. Volume means contracts traded today.
Learn more
Net contract credit
Estimated cash from selling one contract after the estimated fee.
Option price × 100 − estimated fee
Learn more
Return on capital Period return · Contract term
Estimated credit compared with the money backing the trade. It covers this option's life.
Net contract credit ÷ capital required
Learn more
Capital required Return collateral
Estimated money or share value needed to back one contract.
Covered call = underlying price × 100
Cash-secured put = strike × 100 − net contract credit
Learn more
Net sale / breakeven price
Call: your effective sale price. Put: your effective purchase price. Both include premium.
Call net sale = strike + net contract credit ÷ 100
Put breakeven = strike − net contract credit ÷ 100
Learn more
Delta
How much an option price may move when the stock moves $1. Delta changes over time.
Learn more
Implied volatility IV
How big a future move the option market expects. Higher IV often means pricier options.
Learn more
Theta per day
How much value an option may lose in one day just from time passing.
Learn more
Gamma
How fast delta may change when the stock moves $1.
Learn more
Vega
How much an option price may change when implied volatility moves one point.
Learn more
Breakeven
The stock price where the trade makes about $0 at expiration, before taxes.
Learn more
Strike distance
The percentage gap between the strike and stock price. It shows distance, not safety.
Learn more
Estimated fee
The assumed trading charge. Your broker's real fee may be different.
Learn more
Radar calculation inputs Model assumptions
The guesses Radar uses in its math, such as contract size, fees, rates, and dividends.
Learn more
