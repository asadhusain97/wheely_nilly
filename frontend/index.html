How the numbers work

Glossary

A field guide to the portfolio and Radar metrics used throughout Wheely Nilly. Dashboard returns use matched, closed contracts; open credits remain unrealized.

Definitions for portfolio performance, capital, contracts, strategy settings, and Radar terms.

Strategy settings

Goals & ticker changes Settings layers
A goal supplies the full monitoring profile for its strategy. A ticker change replaces one value only for that ticker and strategy.
Effective value = ticker change, otherwise goal value
Goal profiles Keep Shares · Earn Income · Plan Exit · Plan Entry
Editable starting points for different intentions. Keep Shares and Plan Exit apply to covered calls, Plan Entry applies to cash-secured puts, and Earn Income can apply to either. The names organize rules; they are not trading recommendations.
DTE range Time window
The shortest and longest time an option may have before expiration. An option must fall inside both limits to pass.
Minimum DTE ≤ days to expiration ≤ maximum DTE
Moneyness Strike / stock
How the strike price compares with the current stock price. 100% means they are equal; 90% means the strike is 10% below the stock; 110% means it is 10% above.
Moneyness = strike price ÷ stock price × 100%
Target delta range Absolute delta
Keeps options whose delta falls between your chosen limits. Wheely Nilly uses the size of delta, so a put delta of −0.25 is treated as 0.25. A blank limit leaves that side open.
Minimum delta ≤ |option delta| ≤ maximum delta
Maximum spread Liquidity rule
The largest allowed gap between the bid and ask, measured against their midpoint. A smaller gap usually means a more usable quote.
Midpoint = (bid + ask) ÷ 2
Spread % = (ask − bid) ÷ midpoint × 100
Minimum open interest Liquidity rule
The fewest open contracts an option must have to pass. Open interest counts contracts that remain open across the market.
Passes when open interest ≥ minimum
Minimum volume Liquidity rule
The fewest contracts that must have traded during the current session. It filters out options with too little recent trading activity.
Passes when today's volume ≥ minimum
Maximum quote age Freshness
The oldest price quote the screener may use. Older quotes are left out because the market may have moved since they were recorded.
Quote age = current time − quote timestamp
Minimum return One option period
The smallest estimated return accepted over that option's own DTE. It is not an annualized rate. Net premium is the estimated option credit after the estimated fee.
Covered call = net premium ÷ current value of 100 shares
Cash-secured put = net premium ÷ (strike × 100 − net premium)
Net price guard Ticker setting
An optional per-share limit for one ticker. It can set the lowest net sale price for a covered call or the highest net purchase price for a cash-secured put. When monitoring begins using these settings, it will filter choices; it will not place an order.
Covered-call net sale price = strike + net premium per share
Put net purchase price = strike − net premium per share

Performance

Booked profit Booked P&L
The profit or loss from matched, closed short-option contracts after fees. Open premium is not booked until its contract closes.
Opening net credit + closing net cash flow
Return on collateral Return
Booked profit from qualified closed contracts divided by their combined entry collateral. A trade is excluded when its profit, collateral, or holding period cannot be established.
Qualified booked profit ÷ qualified entry collateral
Annualized return Annualized rate
A return scaled to one year. The portfolio dashboard weights qualified closed contracts by collateral and days held; same-day trades count as one day. The screener annualizes each candidate's estimated period return.
Dashboard: qualified booked profit × 365 ÷ Σ(collateral × days held)
Radar: net premium ÷ return collateral × 365 ÷ DTE
Capital velocity
Booked profit produced per $1,000 of collateral over a standardized 30-day period. It measures how efficiently deployed capital has generated realized profit.
Qualified booked profit ÷ collateral-days × 30 × $1,000
Premium capture
The share of positive opening credit retained as booked profit across closed contracts. Losses and buyback costs reduce the result.
Booked profit ÷ positive opening credit on closed contracts
Monthly booked P&L
Booked profit and loss grouped by the month each contract closed, then stacked by ticker.

Capital & contracts

Wheel capital
Capital currently associated with the wheel: cash securing open puts plus the broker cost basis of complete 100-share lots.
Open CSP collateral + share capital
CSP collateral Cash secured
Cash required to secure a short put at its strike. CSP means cash-secured put.
Strike × contract multiplier × open contracts
Share capital Shares committed
The broker-reported per-share cost basis of complete 100-share lots. For a covered call, only the shares committed to its contracts are used as collateral.
Broker cost basis per share × 100-share lots
Collateral Ticker view
Current CSP collateral plus share capital for a ticker. On an individual contract, it means the cash securing a put or the cost basis of shares committed to a call.
Open CSPs / open CCs
The number of current short put or short call contracts. CC means covered call; one standard contract represents 100 shares unless its multiplier says otherwise.
Contract multiplier
The number of shares one option contract controls. Standard equity options generally use 100; calculations use the contract's recorded multiplier when available.
Opening credit
The net cash received when a short option was opened, after recorded fees and allocated to the matched contract quantity.
Closing cash flow Close cost
The net cash from closing a contract. A buy-to-close cost is negative; expiration and assignment contribute zero closing option cash in booked option profit.
DTE Days to expiration
Calendar days remaining until the option expires. Next expiry is the earliest expiration among current short-option contracts.
Stock price
The latest available brokerage quote, falling back to the refreshed position price when a quote is unavailable.

Radar opportunities

Candidate rank
The order of matching contracts. Radar sorts first by closeness to the target delta, then the middle of the DTE range, period return, spread, expiration, and strike.
Strike price
The fixed per-share price at which the option holder may buy shares for a call or sell shares for a put if the option is exercised.
Underlying price
The market-data provider's stock or fund price captured with the option chain and used for Radar calculations.
Executable option price Midpoint · Bid · Ask
The estimated per-share execution price. Bid is the highest displayed buyer price and ask is the lowest displayed seller price. Radar uses their midpoint only after the spread passes the saved liquidity limit.
Midpoint = (bid + ask) ÷ 2
Bid-ask spread Quote width
The percentage gap between the lowest displayed seller price and highest displayed buyer price. Radar measures that gap against their midpoint.
Spread % = (ask − bid) ÷ midpoint × 100
Open interest / volume Liquidity activity
Open interest counts option contracts that remain open across the market. Volume counts contracts traded during the current session.
Net contract credit
The estimated cash received for one standard 100-share contract after the estimated fee.
Net contract credit = executable option price × 100 − estimated fee
Return on capital Period return · Contract term
The candidate's estimated net credit divided by its return collateral for this option period.
Covered call = net contract credit ÷ current value of 100 shares
Cash-secured put = net contract credit ÷ (strike × 100 − net contract credit)
Net sale / purchase price
The effective per-share stock price after the option's estimated net credit. Calls show a net sale price; puts show a net purchase price.
Net sale price = strike + net contract credit ÷ 100
Net purchase price = strike − net contract credit ÷ 100
Delta
The estimated option-price change for a $1 move in the stock, also commonly read as a rough sensitivity measure. Radar calculates it with Black–Scholes when Yahoo supplies implied volatility.
Implied volatility IV
The annualized volatility implied by the option's market price. Higher values mean the market is pricing a wider range of possible stock moves.
Theta per day
The estimated daily change in the option's value from one day passing, with other inputs held constant. Radar calculates it with Black–Scholes when Yahoo supplies implied volatility.
Breakeven
For a put, the strike minus net credit per share. For a call, Radar subtracts net credit per share from broker cost basis when available, otherwise from the current stock price.
Strike distance
The absolute percentage gap between the strike and current stock price.
Strike distance = |strike − stock price| ÷ stock price
Estimated fee
The per-contract fee estimate subtracted from gross option credit. Radar currently assumes $0.65 for one contract.
Radar calculation inputs Model assumptions
Standard contracts use a 100-share multiplier. The Black–Scholes estimate uses a 4.5% risk-free rate and 0% dividend yield. Annualized return uses period return × 365 ÷ DTE.
