Before you connect

Read-only means it cannot trade.

Wheely Nilly asks SnapTrade for read-only access. It can read account balances, positions, and trade history. It cannot place, change, or cancel a trade.

You do not create a Wheely Nilly account. Portfolio snapshots, strategy settings, watchlists, and Radar results stay in your browser, not in a Wheely Nilly database.

How it works

Your brokerage supplies the facts. Your rules shape the answer.

  1. 1

    Connect your brokerage

    SnapTrade handles the secure connection and gives Wheely Nilly read-only access to the brokerage data you approve. The current flow has only been tested with Robinhood.

  2. 2

    Refresh market data

    Wheely Nilly uses Yahoo Finance data through the open-source yfinance library for stock prices, option chains, and quotes. When a contract has implied volatility but no Greeks, the app estimates delta and theta. Yahoo data is unofficial and can be delayed, so confirm live prices with your broker.

  3. 3

    Apply your wheel rules

    The app combines those inputs in your browser. It checks open contracts, ranks covered calls and cash-secured puts, and keeps your saved view available on this device.

Why this exists

The wheel should not require a brokerage tab, an option chain, and a spreadsheet open at the same time.

Wheely Nilly was built to answer the recurring questions that are easy to lose in that shuffle: what is open, how much premium has been captured, whether a contract has reached your close target, and which next trade fits the rules you already chose.

It is an analysis workspace, not an autopilot. The final decision and every trade stay with you.
